Privacy Policy

This draft describes the information HOMEmind may need to operate its intended features. Final collection, storage, and retention practices must match the implemented system.

1. Information that may be collected

  • Account details such as name, email address, contact details, and account role.
  • Technician profile information such as specialty, service area, and experience.
  • Company information, locations, equipment inventories, and authorized team members.
  • Equipment details, purchase and warranty dates, maintenance records, service requests, invoices, and related photos uploaded by a user.
  • Basic technical and security information needed to maintain the service.

2. Why information is used

Information may be used to create and manage accounts, organize equipment and maintenance history, route service requests, send warranty or maintenance reminders, provide reports, calculate service commissions, manage company subscriptions, and protect the platform against misuse.

3. Sharing

Information should be shared only when needed to provide a requested service—for example, sharing relevant request details with the selected technician—or to administer a company account, process a payment, comply with law, or protect users and the service. HOMEmind should not sell personal information. The final policy must identify any actual payment, hosting, analytics, messaging, or AI providers used by the live product.

4. Photos and AI troubleshooting

If users upload equipment photos or describe a fault for troubleshooting, they should avoid including unnecessary personal, financial, or sensitive information. AI-generated suggestions may be inaccurate and should not replace professional assessment for hazardous faults.

5. Security and retention

HOMEmind should use appropriate access controls, secure authentication, and safeguards for stored information. Records should be retained only as long as needed for the stated purposes, legal obligations, dispute handling, and legitimate business operations. Exact technical controls and retention periods must be documented before launch.
