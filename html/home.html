FAIR & TRANSPARENT

A Simple Model That Works for Everyone.

HOMEmind connects customers with technicians while keeping the service pricing transparent. HOMEmind earns a transparent 10% commission on each completed technician service. When the customer pays through HOMEmind, the commission is deducted automatically. If payment is handled outside the platform, the commission remains recorded as due until settled.

Service Price 50 JOD
HOMEmind Commission 5 JOD
Technician Receives 45 JOD

Example: on a 50 JOD completed service, HOMEmind's 10% share is 5 JOD. Online payments will deduct this automatically; for off-platform payments, the commission is tracked as due. Read the Technician Commission Policy.
