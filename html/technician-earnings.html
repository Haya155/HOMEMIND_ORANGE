HOW IT WORKS

The 10% commission

HOMEmind charges 10% of the full approved invoice total (labor + spare parts + other approved charges). If the customer pays through HOMEmind, the commission is deducted automatically and the remaining amount is paid to you. If payment happens outside HOMEmind, the commission is tracked as due until settled. Example for a 100 JOD invoice:
