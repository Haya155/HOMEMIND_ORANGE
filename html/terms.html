Terms & Conditions

These draft terms explain the intended use of the HOMEmind website and services. They must be reviewed for local legal compliance before launch.

1. Using HOMEmind

HOMEmind is designed to help individuals and organizations organize equipment information, maintenance records, warranty dates, service requests, and connections with technicians. Users must provide accurate account information and keep their login details secure.

2. Service requests and technicians

Submitting a request does not guarantee immediate availability, a specific arrival time, a diagnosis, or a repair outcome. Users and technicians should agree on the scope of work, parts, timing, and final invoice before work proceeds.

Technicians are responsible for the quality, safety, legality, and accuracy of the services and invoices they provide. HOMEmind facilitates discovery and service management; it does not replace the parties' agreed service terms.

3. Platform commission

HOMEmind's intended technician commission is 10% of the approved completed-service invoice, including labor and spare parts and any other approved charges. When payment is processed through HOMEmind, the commission is intended to be deducted automatically. If payment occurs outside the platform, the commission is recorded as due and must be settled under the Technician Commission Policy.

See the Technician Commission Policy for the full calculation and payment rules.

4. Company subscriptions

Company features may be limited by the selected plan. Paid plans, billing intervals, renewal, cancellation, and plan limits are described in the Company Subscription Policy and Company Plans. Payments are not enabled in this demo prototype.

5. Acceptable use and availability

  • Do not misuse the platform, submit fraudulent requests, or upload unlawful or unsafe content.
  • Keep equipment and service records accurate and update them when information changes.
  • HOMEmind may update features, correct errors, or temporarily suspend access for maintenance or security.
  • Any disputes should first be raised through the available support channel and handled in accordance with applicable law.

6. No emergency service

HOMEmind is not an emergency response service. For immediate danger, fire, gas leaks, electrical hazards, or threats to life, contact the relevant local emergency service or qualified authority.

7. Changes and contact

These terms may be updated as the product develops. The final production version should show its effective date and provide a verified contact channel for questions or complaints.
