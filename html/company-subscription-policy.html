Company Subscription Policy

These draft subscription rules explain how HOMEmind plans to offer a limited free plan and paid company plans.

1. Available plans

HOMEmind intends to offer a limited Free plan, a paid Business plan, and a custom Enterprise plan. The features and indicative prices shown on the Company Plans page are a prototype proposal and may change before commercial launch.

2. Plan limits and access

Each plan may limit the number of equipment records, locations, team members, reports, or administrative features. The applicable limits should be shown before a company selects or changes a plan.

3. Billing and renewals

Paid subscriptions are intended to be billed monthly or annually as displayed at checkout. Before launch, HOMEmind must clearly disclose the billing amount, currency, renewal date, taxes or additional fees, accepted payment methods, and how to cancel. No paid subscription or automatic renewal is active in this prototype.

4. Cancellations and plan changes

Companies should be able to request cancellation or a plan change through their account or support. The final policy must state when changes take effect, whether refunds or prorated amounts apply, and how access is handled after a paid plan ends.

5. Company data

Ending a subscription should not silently delete company records. The production service should explain any read-only access period, export option, retention period, and deletion process, subject to applicable law.

6. Contact and legal review

This is a draft for the HOMEmind training project. Final billing, cancellation, and consumer/business terms should be reviewed for applicable local law and must match the actual subscription system.
