Technician Commission Policy

This page explains the intended 10% commission model before a technician registers. The final agreement should be accepted before technicians can receive real paid requests.

1. Commission rate

HOMEmind commission: 10% of the full approved invoice for each completed service, including labor, spare parts, and other charges agreed with the customer.

2. Example calculation

For an approved invoice of 100 JOD: HOMEmind commission is 10 JOD and the technician's share is 90 JOD before the technician's own expenses, taxes, or other obligations.

3. Automatic deduction when platform payment is available

When a customer pays through HOMEmind's integrated payment system, the 10% commission is intended to be deducted automatically from the completed service payment before the technician's remaining share is settled.

If a customer pays outside HOMEmind, or the integrated payment feature is not available, the commission will be shown as due in the technician's account and must be settled using the payment process HOMEmind communicates. The website prototype does not currently process or deduct real payments.

4. Completion, invoice, and disputes

  • The technician must submit an accurate invoice and mark the service complete.
  • The commission is calculated from the approved final invoice, not from an estimate that was never charged.
  • Cancelled services with no completed work should not incur a completed-service commission; refunds, partial completion, and disputed invoices must be reviewed and adjusted fairly.
  • Technicians must not falsify invoices or bypass the platform to avoid an agreed commission on a service introduced through HOMEmind.

5. Records and statements

The technician workspace should show each service invoice, the 10% calculation, deduction or payment status, adjustments, and any outstanding balance. A statement should distinguish amounts deducted automatically from amounts still due.

6. Acceptance and updates

Technicians should review and accept this policy before completing registration. Material changes should be communicated and accepted where required. The final policy must specify supported payment methods, settlement timing, dispute handling, and any applicable tax treatment.
